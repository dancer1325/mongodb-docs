# Open a Saved Pipeline in Atlas

You can create new pipelines based on previously saved aggregation pipelines. Saved aggregation pipelines can be loaded and edited in Atlas to build new pipelines.

## Before You Begin

If you don't have a saved pipeline, you must first:

- Create an aggregation pipeline.
- Save your aggregation pipeline.

## Steps

1. **Click the folder *[icon: Folder]* icon.**

   In the top-left corner of the pipeline builder, click the *[icon: Folder]* folder icon to open the drop-down menu of saved pipelines.

1. **Select an aggregation pipeline.**

   In the drop-down menu, hover over the pipeline you want to open and click **Open**.

1. **Confirm your selection.**

   In the pop-up modal, click **Open Pipeline**.

To learn more, see viewing saved aggregations for details.
