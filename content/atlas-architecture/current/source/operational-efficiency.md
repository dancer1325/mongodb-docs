# Operational Efficiency in the Atlas Well-Architected Framework

The Operational Efficiency pillar of the Atlas [Well-Architected Framework](https://www.mongodb.com/resources/products/capabilities/well-architected-framework) includes features that support your organization's operations with minimum expenditure of time and effort, including tools for automation and monitoring and alert tools and integrations. Use the following Atlas Architecture Center resources to learn about operational efficiency in Atlas:

- [Automation](https://mongodb.com/docs/atlas/architecture/automation/) — Automate resource deployment by integrating with Infrastructure as Code (IaC) tools to configure templates for your processes.
- [Monitoring and Alerts](https://mongodb.com/docs/atlas/architecture/monitoring-alerts/) — Monitor system performance, detect issues, and integrate with your third-party observability and alerting stack. Get recommendations on the alerts you should configure.
- [Development and Testing](https://mongodb.com/docs/atlas/architecture/dev-test/) — Configure a local deployment with the Atlas CLI for development and testing.
